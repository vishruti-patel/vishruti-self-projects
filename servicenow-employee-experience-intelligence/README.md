# ServiceNow Employee Experience Intelligence (VOE Analytics)

## Goal
Analyze ServiceNow-style case data to identify employee friction points and quantify their impact on:
- Resolution time
- SLA breaches
- Reopens
- Employee satisfaction (VOE)

## Why it matters
Internal support quality directly impacts employee productivity and experience. This project surfaces actionable insights for HR and operations leaders and defines governance standards to ensure reporting trust.

## Deliverables
- Leadership dashboard (case volume, resolution time, SLA, VOE)
- Operational dashboard (backlog, aging, reassignment/reopen patterns)
- Governance artifacts (metric definitions + data quality checks)
- Insights summary with recommended actions
